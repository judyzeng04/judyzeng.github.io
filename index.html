A bit about me

the short version

I studied statistics and political science at McGill, which mostly taught me that numbers tell you a lot, and people tell you the rest. I took that to the tech side of banking at CIBC, and now to project management at SAP.

On most teams, I end up as the go-between, making sure the people asking for something and the people building it actually understand each other. I pick things up fast, and I'm as comfortable in the details as I am in the big picture. I've led teams of 20 at McMUN and helped present youth policy at the United Nations.

I'm also just very curious. I'm always learning something new, and I like coming at problems from a creative angle, especially the ones without an obvious answer.
